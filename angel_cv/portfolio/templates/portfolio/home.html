Ángel Domínguez

Data Analyst | MSc in Space Engineering | ESA intern

I studied a Bachelor's degree in Materials Engineering (bilingual) at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), including a semester abroad at Michigan State University. My Final Degree Project, consisted on evaluating the corrosive behavior of electrodes exposed to non-thermal plasma in atmospheric air.

Afterwards I completed a Master's degree in Space Engineering at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M). My Master Thesis, consisted on the 3D design and study of lunar landing pads using CFD analysis software.

My career started on the engineering side: an internship as Mechanical & Physics Engineer at Capgemini Engineering, an internship as Hardware Engineer at the European Space Agency working on the Electronic Fieldbook project at the European Astronaut Center, and a role as Mechanical & Thermal Engineer at Thales Alenia Space.

From there I moved into data, first as a Data & Engineer Analyst at Capgemini Engineering, applying Python and machine learning to structural and thermal calculations, and currently as a Data Analyst at Prima Assicurazioni, building data pipelines and reporting tools for the claims business in Spain.

I combine an analytical engineering foundation with hands-on experience in Python, SQL and BI tools, and I'm currently completing a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics on the side.
